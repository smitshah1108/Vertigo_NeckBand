Background

What is vertigo?

Vertigo is a symptom, not a disease — the false sensation of spinning or movement. It's caused by underlying conditions and can lead to dangerous falls. This device is a non-invasive short-term companion to treatment.

vertere
Latin — "to turn"
↓
vertigo
"a whirling" — false sensation of movement
Common causes
BPPV — benign paroxysmal positional vertigo
Ménière's disease — inner ear fluid disorder
Vestibular neuritis — nerve inflammation
Treatment is surgery or therapy — Vertigo Band is a non-invasive short-term companion to either.

Intelligence

Machine learning on the edge

Instead of a fixed threshold, the ESP32 runs a TFLite Micro model trained on each patient's personal movement data — personalized medicine on a microcontroller.

Phase 01 · Calibration

Label your own episodes

During the calibration period, the patient presses a button on the neckband when they experience a real vertigo episode. That labels the IMU data as a genuine trigger.

Phase 02 · Training

Pi trains overnight

The Raspberry Pi trains a personalized model using Edge Impulse, learning the difference between real triggers and normal actions like sleeping, stretching, or looking up.

Phase 03 · Inference

ESP32 runs on-device

The trained TFLite Micro model is flashed to the ESP32. Inference runs locally — zero latency, no internet, no privacy concerns. The Pi re-trains periodically to improve accuracy.

Power System

Zero-downtime hot-swap batteries

A medical device can't run out of power mid-day. Two LiPo batteries wired through Schottky diodes keep the system alive continuously — swap one while the other feeds the ESP32.

⚡ Hot-swap circuit

Two 3.7V 1000mAh LiPo cells connected in parallel via 1N5819 Schottky diodes. Whichever has higher voltage feeds the system. Swap the external cell while the internal one keeps everything running.

🔌 Dual TP4056 charging

Each LiPo has its own TP4056 charging module. They charge independently via USB-C, so both batteries can be topped up simultaneously without any downtime to the system.

🧮 Runtime estimate

ESP32 + TFLite inference + Wi-Fi burst mode draws ~80–100mA average. Two 1000mAh cells give approximately 8–10 hours continuous wear — well beyond a full patient day.

📢 WRO talking point

"Unlike most wearables, this device has zero downtime — critical for a medical device that must protect the patient 24/7, not just when it's charged."

Evaluation

Strengths & limitations

✓ Project strengths

Non-invasive and immediate — warns before the fall, not after
Personalized ML model adapts to each patient's unique trigger pattern
Longitudinal data logging lets doctors track daily trigger frequency
Fully local — no cloud, no subscription, privacy preserved
Comfortable soft neckband design, suitable for long-term wear
Zero downtime via hot-swappable dual-LiPo power system

! Known challenges

ESP32 battery drain is higher with ML inference running
Calibration period required before model reaches full accuracy
Initial false positives possible until sufficient labelled data is collected
Wi-Fi range limited to same network as Raspberry Pi
Neckband comfort varies by patient neck size and sensitivity

WRO 2026 Theme

Robots Meet Culture

Vertigo Band isn't just a medical device — it's a guardian of India's living cultural heritage, protecting the human artists who carry it forward.

The Problem Nobody Talks About

India's classical performers are at risk — from within

Bharatanatyam. Kathak. Kuchipudi. Manipuri. India's classical dance forms are UNESCO-recognized cultural heritage — but the artists who practice them spend years executing intense, repetitive head and neck movements. Without real-time feedback, incorrect angles and overextension cause cumulative injury that ends careers silently. When a dancer stops, centuries of embodied tradition stop with them. Vertigo Band is a wearable robotic co-creator — a technological partner that watches, warns, and protects so the art never has to stop.

Area 2 — Primary fit
Co-Creation: Humans, Robots & AI
WRO defines this area as robots that "support artists, musicians, and performers — not replace their skill or creativity." Vertigo Band is exactly that: a real-time robotic partner that senses the dancer's movement, runs on-device ML inference, and intervenes only when the body is at risk. The artist leads; the robot protects.
